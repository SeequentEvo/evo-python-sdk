evo.compute.outputs.ResultNode

One object in a task's result payload, viewed through its results schema.

Nested objects and arrays of objects are hydrated into further nodes, so the whole result is reachable by attribute access. Nodes annotated output: geoscience-object or output: attribute additionally offer load and to_dataframe.
